Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

PageDye helps you customize website backgrounds and visual presentation. This policy explains what stays on your device and what may be sent when you explicitly use optional AI features.

Information stored locally

Normal settings, local images, videos, themes, site rules, custom effects, AI settings, prompts and conversations are stored in your browser on your device. PageDye has no account system, advertising, analytics or telemetry.

Optional AI features

AI is optional and is called only after you configure a provider and actively request it. Depending on your settings, PageDye may send your API key, the current hostname and path, page color and layout features or selected selectors, your prompts and conversations, images you actively attach, and the current local background image when visual analysis is enabled. PageDye does not send page body text, form inputs or passwords.

Service providers

Requests go only to the Anthropic, OpenAI-compatible, or custom endpoint that you choose. The selected provider handles submitted information under its own privacy policy and terms. Custom endpoints may have additional security and privacy risks; use only endpoints you trust.

Remote images and URLs

If you choose a remote image or URL, your browser must contact that source to load it. The source may receive standard network information such as your IP address according to the source’s own policies.

Security and control

PageDye uses secure transport when supported by the endpoint. API keys are stored locally and are not included in PageDye backups. You can disable AI, remove providers, delete conversations and clear PageDye data at any time through the extension settings.

Data sharing

PageDye does not sell user data. Information is sent to a provider only when required for an AI request that you initiate, or to a remote image or URL that you choose to load.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, please use GitHub Issues: GitHub Issues
